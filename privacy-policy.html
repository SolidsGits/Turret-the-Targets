This Privacy Policy explains how SolishStudios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Turret the Targets (the "App").

Turret the Targets does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, telephone number, or other directly identifying information in order to play the game.

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. As part of providing advertising services, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information from your device.

This may include:

Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.
